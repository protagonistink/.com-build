# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Artists, founders and organizational leaders contemplating a rebrand, website, pitch or campaign. They have substantial work behind them and ambition for what comes next. Some arrive with a clear direction; others need help articulating it. They're evaluating whether this practice can do the work and whether its judgment can be trusted.

## Product Purpose

The site for Protagonist Ink, a narrative strategy, messaging and copywriting practice. It shows what the practice can be hired to do, demonstrates evidence of judgment and craft, and makes an incomplete brief feel welcome. Success is a visitor who doesn't have to diagnose themselves as "needing narrative strategy" before reaching out.

## Brand Personality

Intelligent, culturally engaged, human, precise, assured. Creative authorship alongside practical clarity. A contemporary editorial studio: expressive typography, carefully composed photography, the clarity of a well-edited publication. The methodology is not the visual subject; story emerges through sequence, emphasis and language, not through illustrating the craft literally.

## Anti-references

No invented clients, projects, testimonials, metrics or outcomes; unresolved copy stays bracketed rather than faked. No literal literary or process props as brand devices: book pages, typewriters, screenplay formatting, blueprints, ink splashes, red-pen annotations, chapter metaphors. No desk photography, no anonymous single-portrait stand-ins, no smiling-creative-team stock, no decorative ink, no architecture-as-rigour.

## Design Principles

- The site never claims what it can't back up. No invented clients, projects, testimonials or metrics; anything unresolved stays visibly bracketed rather than papered over.
- The site itself is the demonstration. Its own craft and judgment are the evidence for what the practice can do, so the execution has to hold up to the same standard being sold.
- A visitor with a half-formed brief should feel invited in, not made to diagnose their own need first. The path to "start a conversation" stays open and low-friction even for someone who isn't sure yet.
- Story is carried through sequence, emphasis and language, not through literal props of the writing process. The methodology is not the visual subject.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target: 4.5:1 text contrast (red text never directly on ink), visible focus states, 44px touch targets, descriptive alt text. All motion must pause or be removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Motion stays limited to the hero, except the How we work stages easing open and shut on hover, focus or tap; no typewriter effects, rotating words, scroll-jacking, parallax, custom cursors, intros or autoplay sound anywhere on the site.
